Theme 6: Visibility and Collective Validation 
 
Key Finding 
 
Participants expressed enthusiasm for making friction visible across departments. 
 
Concepts discussed: 
• Issue dashboards 
• Heat maps 
• Word clouds 
• Friction trends 
• Community visibility 
• Up-voting mechanisms 
 
Tension Identified 
 
Benefit: 
• Identifies genuine recurring issues. 
• Builds consensus. 
 
Risk: 
• Encourages bandwagon moaning. 
• Amplifies perception rather than reality. 
 
Theme 7: Importance of Context and Interpretation 
 
Key Finding 
 
Participants repeatedly stressed that raw reporting data alone is insufficient. 
 
They argued that: 
• A single report may identify a major systemic issue. 
• Ten reports may reflect a local irritation. 
• Interpretation matters more than volume. 
 
Design Implication 
 
The discussion strongly supports combining: 
• Quantitative signals 
• Qualitative interpretation 
• Expert review 
 
Theme 8: Simplicity is Critical 
 
Key Finding 
 
Participants repeatedly requested: 
• Mobile-first access 
• Voice-to-text capability 
• Minimal data entry 
• Low cognitive effort 
• Fast submission 
 
One contributor explicitly stated that reporters should be able to submit frustrations without needing to analyse causes, impacts or solutions. 
 
Overarching Insight 
 
The meeting fundamentally reinforced the core Friction Intelligence Platform hypothesis: 
 
Frontline staff possess large amounts of operational intelligence that organisations currently fail to capture, aggregate and act upon. 
 
The strongest recurring themes were: 
1. Small frictions create large organisational costs. 
2. Emotional burden is a significant consequence of friction. 
3. Trust and feedback are prerequisites for engagement. 
4. Simplicity is essential for adoption. 
5. Visibility enables learning and validation. 
6. Culture, not technology alone, will determine success. 
 
The discussion suggests that the success of FIP will depend less on its technical capability and more on its ability to create a trusted mechanism that converts everyday frustrations into visible, actionable organisational intelligence.
